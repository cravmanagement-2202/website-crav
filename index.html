FAQ

Pricing Questions

Is there a minimum contract period?
We recommend a 3-month engagement for meaningful results. After that, it's fully month-to-month with 30 days notice to cancel.
Are ad spends included in the pricing?
No — ad spend is separate and goes directly to Meta/Google. Our fee covers campaign management, creative, and optimization. We work with budgets from ₹10K/month.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes, absolutely. You can change plans at the start of any billing cycle. We'll prorate any differences.
Do you offer discounts for startups or NGOs?
Yes! We have special rates for early-stage startups and non-profits. Book a call and mention it — we'll work something out.
